## 1. Who we are
NovaCRM B.V. is the **data controller** for the personal data described below. You can contact our **Data Protection Officer** at dpo@novacrm.example or by post at the address above. For customers in Québec, our **Person in charge of the protection of personal information** (Privacy Officer) is the VP of Trust, reachable at privacy-officer@novacrm.example (also published at novacrm.example/privacy).

## 2. What we collect, why, and our lawful basis
| Data category | Purpose (specific) | Lawful basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, business email | Create and administer your account | Performance of a contract — Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Billing details | Process payments and issue invoices | Legal obligation — Art. 6(1)(c) |
| Product usage logs | Diagnose errors and maintain service reliability | Legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Marketing email address | Send product newsletters (only if you opt in) | Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) |

We collect **only the data necessary** for each purpose above and do not repurpose it incompatibly *(data minimisation, purpose limitation)*.

## 3. Recipients
We share personal data only with: (a) our cloud hosting provider (EU region); (b) our payment processor; and (c) our email-delivery sub-processor. Each is bound by a written data-processing agreement. A current sub-processor list is at novacrm.example/subprocessors.

## 4. International transfers
Where data is transferred outside the EEA (our email sub-processor operates in the United States), the transfer is protected by the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** plus supplementary measures. For Québec data communicated outside the province, we complete a **Privacy Impact Assessment** beforehand.

## 5. Retention
We retain account data for the life of your subscription plus **24 months**, billing records for **7 years** (tax law), and marketing data until you withdraw consent. After these periods data is securely deleted.

## 6. Your rights
You have the right to **access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object** to processing of your personal data, and to **withdraw consent** at any time. You also have the right to **lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority** (e.g., the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec). Exercise any right at privacy@novacrm.example; we respond within one month.

## 7. Privacy by default (Québec Law 25 s. 9.1)
New accounts ship with the **highest-confidentiality settings enabled by default** — analytics sharing off, profile private — with no action required by you.

## 8. Security (GDPR Art. 32)
We protect personal data with **AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit**, role-based access control with least privilege, multi-factor authentication for staff, pseudonymisation of analytics data, annual penetration testing, and a documented incident-response plan. We maintain a **register of confidentiality incidents** and notify affected individuals and the relevant authority where a risk of serious injury exists.

## 9. Records of processing
We maintain records of processing activities under GDPR Art. 30, reviewed annually.

Privacy questions: privacy@novacrm.example · DPO: dpo@novacrm.example
