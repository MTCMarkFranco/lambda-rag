# Building Permit Application — Distribution Warehouse, Mississauga, ON

A new 28,500 m² Class A distribution warehouse on a greenfield industrial
site in Mississauga. This permit application addresses Ontario Building
Code conformance, accessibility under AODA, federal Impact Assessment Act
screening, and Indigenous consultation.

## Project Description

A single-storey distribution warehouse (Group F, Division 2 occupancy)
with attached two-storey office (Group D occupancy). The building is of
non-combustible construction with steel frame and tilt-up concrete
panels.

## Accessibility — AODA / IASR Compliance

All exterior paths of travel are designed in accordance with IASR
O. Reg. 191/11, Part IV.1 (Design of Public Spaces Standards). The
accessible path of travel from the public sidewalk to the principal
entrance is a minimum 1.65 m clear width with a maximum running slope of
1:20 and a stable, slip-resistant surface. The visitor parking area
includes two Type A and two Type B accessible spaces, with signage
conforming to IASR s. 80.36. The principal entrance is power-operated
per IASR s. 80.13. The application explicitly attests to AODA / IASR
conformance for all elements within the regulation's scope.

## Fire Egress

Per OBC 3.4.2, the maximum travel distance from any point in the
warehouse floor area to the nearest exit is 31.0 m, within the 45 m
allowable for sprinklered Group F-2 occupancies. Two-hour fire-rated
exit stairs serve the office mezzanine. Travel distance to an exit door
on the office level is 18.0 m. The application confirms full
conformance with OBC 3.4.2.

## Fire Suppression — Sprinkler System

A fully-supervised wet-pipe sprinkler system designed and installed in
accordance with NFPA 13 is provided throughout the warehouse and
office. Hazard classification is Extra Hazard Group 2 (rack storage).
Sprinkler protection complies with OBC 3.2.2. A two-stage fire
alarm system per CAN/ULC-S524 is provided.

## Stormwater Management

Stormwater management is designed in accordance with the City of
Mississauga *Stormwater Management Policy* and MECP guidelines, including
an oil-grit separator and an on-site detention pond sized for the
100-year storm event. A construction stormwater (SWPPP) is included.

## Environmental Impact Assessment — Federal Screening

A federal designated-project screening has been completed under the
Impact Assessment Act, S.C. 2019, c. 28. The proposed warehouse is
not a "designated project" listed in the Physical Activities
Regulations (SOR/2019-285), and accordingly no federal impact assessment
is triggered. The proponent has filed a screening memo with IAAC for the
record. This determination is documented in the *Federal IAA Screening
Memo* (Appendix C).

## Indigenous Consultation and Accommodation

The proponent acknowledges the Crown's duty to consult under section
35 of the Constitution Act, 1982 and *Haida Nation v. British Columbia
(Minister of Forests)*, 2004 SCC 73. A consultation log records
engagement with the Mississaugas of the Credit First Nation, whose
treaty territory includes the project lands. The First Nation has been
provided with the application package and a 30-day comment window.
Accommodations agreed to include a pre-construction archaeological
assessment under the Ontario Heritage Act and a community-benefits
contribution. Engagement is logged in the *Consultation and
Accommodation Log* (Appendix D).
