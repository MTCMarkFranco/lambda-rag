# Master Services Agreement — Canadian Commercial Customer / Canadian-Law Vendor

> **Synthetic / public-domain only.** Authored for the lambda-rag golden
> corpus as a positive-control reference contract.

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between **North Bay
Manufacturing Ltd.** ("Customer"), a corporation incorporated under
the laws of Ontario, and **Cedarwood Technologies Inc.** ("Provider"),
a corporation incorporated under the laws of Canada.

## 2. Term

The initial term is 24 months from the Effective Date with auto-renewal
for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 60 days'
written notice.

## 3. Payment and Compensation

Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days
(net 30) of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payments accrue interest
at the rate published by the Bank of Canada plus 1% per annum.

## 4. Privacy and Data Processing

Provider shall process Personal Information only as required to deliver
the Services and in compliance with the *Personal Information
Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and any substantially
similar provincial privacy legislation applicable to the data subjects,
including Quebec's Law 25 where applicable.

## 5. Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed
the fees paid by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months
preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable
for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material uncured breach
on thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer may terminate this
Agreement for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and
the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. The exclusive venue for
any dispute is the courts of Ontario.
