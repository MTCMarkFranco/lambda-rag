## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between **Maple Leaf
Logistics Inc.** ("Customer"), a Canadian corporation, and **Pinewood
Software Holdings LLC** ("Provider"), a Delaware limited liability
company.

## 2. Term

The initial term is 36 months from the Effective Date with auto-renewal
for successive 12-month terms unless terminated.

## 3. Payment and Compensation

Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within forty-five (45) days
of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month. All fees
are stated in USD.

## 4. Privacy and Data Processing

Provider shall process Personal Data in accordance with the General
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and shall implement appropriate
technical and organisational measures consistent with industry
standards.

## 5. Liability

Each party shall be responsible for damages caused by its own gross
negligence or wilful misconduct. The parties' respective liabilities
shall be determined under applicable law.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material uncured breach
upon thirty (30) days' written notice. The Provider may, at its sole
discretion, terminate this Agreement immediately upon non-payment by
Customer of any invoice.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware,
without regard to its conflicts-of-law rules. The exclusive venue is
the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
