## Your privacy matters to us
At DataFlow Analytics, we take your privacy and security very seriously. This notice explains how we handle your information when you use our website analytics dashboard.

## Information we collect
We collect the following information:
- Your name and email address
- Your company name and job title
- Your IP address and approximate location
- Precise device geolocation
- Your browsing activity across the sites where our analytics tag is installed
- Usage data about how you interact with our dashboard

We use this information to improve our services and for general business purposes.

## How we share information
We share your information with our trusted partners and service providers who help us operate the platform. Some of these providers are located in other countries.

## Cookies
We use cookies and similar technologies to provide and enhance our service. By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

## Your choices

## Security
