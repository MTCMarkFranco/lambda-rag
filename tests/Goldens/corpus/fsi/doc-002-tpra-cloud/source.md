# Third-Party Risk Assessment — Cloud SaaS Onboarding (BetaBank Treasury)

> **Synthetic / public-domain only.** Authored for the lambda-rag golden
> corpus.

## 1. Engagement Summary

This document records BetaBank's third-party risk assessment for the
proposed onboarding of NorthStar Treasury Cloud, a SaaS treasury and
liquidity management platform. The engagement has been classified as a
**critical third-party arrangement** under BetaBank's policies.

## 2. Outsourcing — Governance and Third-Party Risk Management Framework

This arrangement is governed by BetaBank's enterprise-wide Third-Party
Risk Management Framework (TPRMF), under which it has been risk-tiered
"Tier 1 — Critical" by the Vendor Governance Committee. Criticality has
been re-confirmed annually since onboarding, and the arrangement is
included in the Bank's quarterly TPRMF reporting to the Risk Committee
of the Board.

## 3. Subcontracting and Outsourcing of Services

NorthStar may engage cloud-infrastructure and observability
subcontractors as necessary to deliver the SaaS platform. The current
subcontractor inventory is reviewed by the Bank annually. NorthStar
will keep BetaBank informed of changes through the standard quarterly
service review.

## 4. Audit and Examination — Vendor Risk Oversight

The contract preserves audit and examination rights for BetaBank, its
internal and external auditors, and OSFI, upon reasonable notice. The
Provider shall make personnel, records, and operational systems
relevant to the Services available for examination by such parties,
including in the context of an OSFI supervisory review.

## 5. Business Continuity

A documented business continuity plan governs the Services. NorthStar
commits to a Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of 4 hours and a Recovery
Point Objective (RPO) of 15 minutes for the production treasury
workload. BCP exercises are conducted semi-annually with BetaBank
participation.

## 6. Operational Resilience — Exit Plan and Transition

A documented exit plan is maintained by the Bank for this critical
arrangement, with invocation triggers covering both planned exit (e.g.
contract non-renewal, change of strategic direction) and stressed exit
(e.g. provider insolvency, sustained service degradation). The exit
plan includes 180 days of transition assistance and a certified data
return / destruction obligation.

## 7. Cybersecurity Incident Response

NorthStar will notify BetaBank within four (4) hours of confirming any
security incident materially affecting BetaBank data or service
availability. Notification will be supplemented with a written
post-incident report within 30 days.
