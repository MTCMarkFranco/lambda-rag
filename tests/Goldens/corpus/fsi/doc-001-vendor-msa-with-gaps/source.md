# Critical Vendor Master Services Agreement — ACME Bank / GlobalCorePay Inc.

> **Synthetic / public-domain only.** This document is not derived from
> any real MSA. It is a deliberately gap-laden first-draft commercial
> agreement for the lambda-rag golden corpus.

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between
**ACME Bank of Canada** (the "Bank"), a federally regulated bank, and
**GlobalCorePay Inc.** (the "Provider"), a payment-processing service
provider.

## 2. Term

The initial term of this Agreement is sixty (60) months from the
Effective Date, with automatic renewal for successive 24-month terms
absent 180 days' written notice of non-renewal.

## 3. Outsourcing — Third-Party Risk Acknowledgement

The Bank acknowledges that this Agreement constitutes a third-party
arrangement under applicable Canadian regulatory expectations. The Bank
maintains policies for managing its third-party relationships at an
enterprise level.

## 4. Subcontractors and Outsourcing

The Provider may subcontract any portion of the Services to any
affiliate or qualified third party. The Provider remains responsible
for the performance of its subcontractors. A current list of material
subcontractors is available on request.

## 5. Audit Rights — Vendor Risk Oversight

Once per calendar year, on at least sixty (60) days' written notice and
during normal business hours, the Bank's internal audit team may inspect
the Provider's records and systems strictly relevant to the Services.
Such inspections shall not unreasonably disrupt the Provider's
operations.

## 6. Business Continuity

The Provider shall maintain a written business continuity plan ("BCP")
covering the Services. The Provider shall test the BCP annually and
shall provide the Bank with a summary of the test results on request.

## 7. Cybersecurity

The Provider shall implement commercially reasonable information
security controls. The Provider shall promptly notify the Bank of any
confirmed material security incident affecting the Services or Bank
data.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material uncured breach
on 60 days' written notice. The Bank may terminate for convenience on
180 days' written notice subject to payment of an early-termination
charge.
