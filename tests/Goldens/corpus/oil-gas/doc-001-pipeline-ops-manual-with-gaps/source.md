# Operations Manual — Acme Pipeline Co. NPS 24 Crude Oil System

This operations manual governs the Acme NPS 24 crude oil system between
Hardisty, AB and the U.S. border. It is filed under the Canadian Energy
Regulator Onshore Pipeline Regulations (SOR/99-294).

## Pipeline Integrity Management Program

Acme operates a pipeline integrity management program (PIM) consistent
with CSA Z662 and CER OPR. The program covers:

- threat identification (external corrosion, internal corrosion,
  geohazards, third-party damage, manufacturing defects)
- ILI scheduling on a five-year cadence
- monitoring via cathodic protection surveys, close-interval surveys,
  and pressure cycling analysis
- corrective-action triggers tied to API 1163 ILI tool tolerance.

## Emergency Response Plan

The emergency response plan (ERP) is maintained in accordance with CER
OPR s. 32 and reviewed annually. ICS structure, notification matrix,
and tier-1/2/3 spill response are documented in the ERP and exercised
twice per year.

## Asset Integrity

Mechanical integrity is managed under risk-based inspection (RBI) per
API 580 / 581 for above-ground facilities. RBI intervals are reviewed
every three years.

## Decommissioning

End-of-life decommissioning will be addressed at retirement under a
future abandonment plan to be filed with the regulator. The current
operations manual does not address financial assurance.

## Spill Prevention

A facility response plan (FRP) and SPCC-equivalent secondary
containment are provided at all above-ground tanks and pump stations.
Inspection cadence is monthly.
