# Operations & Compliance Program — Northstar NPS 12 Sweet Gas Gathering System

This program document governs the Northstar NPS 12 sweet gas gathering
system in Northeast British Columbia and is filed under CER OPR
(SOR/99-294) and applicable BCER directives.

## Pipeline Integrity Management Program

The pipeline integrity management program (PIM) implements CER OPR
s. 40 in full. The program documents:

- threat-identification (CSA Z662 Annex H threat list)
- annual ILI for high-consequence segments (cleanliness + MFL + caliper
  per API 1163)
- monitoring via 12-month close-interval cathodic protection surveys
- corrective-action triggers with documented engineering critical
  assessment under API 579 / CSA Z662 Clause 10.

## Emergency Response Plan — Emergency Management Program

A documented emergency management program (EMP / ERP) is maintained per
CER OPR s. 32. The Northstar EMP includes:

- ICS-based command structure
- a 24/7 notification matrix to the CER, BCER, AER (where applicable),
  the local Indigenous nations, and municipal first responders
- tier-1/2/3 spill response with mutual-aid agreements
- two full-scale exercises annually plus four desktop exercises.

For Alberta-side facilities the program is harmonised with AER
Directive 071.

## Methane Management

The Northstar methane management program implements the federal
Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release of Methane and
Certain Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas Sector),
SOR/2018-66. The program covers:

- a leak-detection-and-repair (LDAR) program with quarterly screening
  using OGI cameras at all compressor stations
- repair timelines that meet or exceed the 30-day requirement under
  SOR/2018-66
- venting limits at all controlled devices (pneumatic instruments,
  glycol dehydrators, compressor seals)
- methane intensity reporting per OGMP 2.0 reporting framework.

Annual fugitive emissions inventory is filed with ECCC.

## Decommissioning / Abandonment

The decommissioning and abandonment plan is filed and updated annually.
Financial assurance is provided through the CER abandonment-funding
mechanism for federally-regulated segments, and through Liability
Management Rating (LMR) compliance with the relevant provincial
regulator for provincial segments. The current funded amount exceeds
the estimated abandonment liability per the most recent third-party
engineering estimate.

## Indigenous Consultation and Accommodation

Northstar acknowledges the Crown's duty to consult under section 35
of the Constitution Act, 1982 (*Haida Nation v. British Columbia
(Minister of Forests)*, 2004 SCC 73; *Tsleil-Waututh Nation v. Canada*,
2018 FCA 153). The Indigenous consultation log records bilateral
engagement with Treaty 8 First Nations whose territory the gathering
system traverses, including:

- pre-construction archaeological assessments under each nation's own
  cultural-heritage protocol
- a community benefits agreement with revenue-sharing terms
- ongoing quarterly engagement meetings.

The consultation file is maintained by the Indigenous Affairs lead and
reviewed by Crown counsel for adequacy.
