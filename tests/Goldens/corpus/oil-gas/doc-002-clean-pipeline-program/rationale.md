# Rationale — `doc-002-clean-pipeline-program`

## What this document tests

A clean, integrated operations & compliance program addressing every
mandatory rule in the corpus by name. The "all-Pass" reference document
for this vertical.

## Expected pattern (locked into `expected-verdict.json`)

All five rules should resolve to **Pass** on at least one matched
section:

- `OG-PIM-001` — *Pipeline Integrity Management Program* names CER OPR
  s. 40.
- `OG-EMERGENCY-001` — *Emergency Management Program* names CER OPR
  s. 32 and AER Directive 071.
- `OG-METHANE-001` — *Methane Management* names SOR/2018-66 and LDAR.
- `OG-DECOM-001` — *Decommissioning / Abandonment* describes both the
  plan and the financial-assurance mechanism.
- `OG-INDIG-001` — *Indigenous Consultation and Accommodation* names
  duty to consult, s. 35, *Haida Nation*, and *Tsleil-Waututh*.
