# Master Services Agreement

## 1. Parties
This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Acme Corp.** ("Customer") and **Vendor Inc.** ("Vendor").

## 2. Term
The initial term of this Agreement is **24 months** from the Effective Date. Renewal occurs automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides written notice 60 days before expiry.

## 3. Payment Terms
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within **45 days** of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month. All fees are stated in USD.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect Confidential Information of the other for a period of 5 years. Disclosure is permitted only to personnel with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations.

## 5. Data Protection
Vendor shall process Customer Personal Data only as instructed and shall implement security measures consistent with ISO 27001. Sub-processors require Customer's prior written consent.

## 6. Liability
Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the **fees paid in the twelve months** preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach uncured after 30 days' written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience on 90 days' notice.

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to conflicts of law principles. The exclusive venue is Wilmington, Delaware.
